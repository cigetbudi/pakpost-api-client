# PakPost V7.4 — Manage Roles & Permissions UI

Incremental patch on top of **V7.3 QA Sandbox Mode** (`pakpost-v73-qa-sandbox-from-v721.patch`).

## What's changed

- Compact **Members & Roles** panel under Workspace → Members.
- Member name/email search and role filter (using PakPost RetroSelect).
- Member totals and per-role counters; multi-role users count once in the total, once per role they hold.
- Collapsible **View access** panel, showing the member's `permissions` received from backend, grouped by Workspace, People & Teams, Collections, Environments, Requests. Unknown future permissions are displayed without claiming they are blocked.
- Collapsible **Manage roles** editor for authorized actors only; Save disabled when selection is unchanged or empty.
- **Add existing member** is collapsed by default; uses existing member API, not invitation email delivery.
- Founder and self-management restrictions retained; Workspace Admin cannot assign privileged roles.
- Role editor, filters and expanded panels reset when selecting a new workspace.
- Asynchronous member writes are guarded against stale workspace/account responses.

## Manual acceptance test

1. As Team Lead, go to Workspace → Members and check summary totals.
2. Search by name/email; filter QA; ensure results are for the current Workspace only.
3. Open View access on QA; confirm `collection.write` and `environment.write` are denied, `collection.read` and `request.execute` allowed (as returned by backend).
4. Assign QA + Technical Writer to an eligible test member; save; refresh. Confirm both role badges and backend permissions are still correct.
5. Reopen Manage roles without changes; Save should be disabled.
6. As Workspace Admin + Developer, confirm you can manage eligible operational-role members, but cannot edit a privileged member or assign Team Lead/Workspace Admin.
7. Confirm Founder and your own membership cannot be reassigned from this interface.
8. Change Workspace while an editor is open; confirm editor, search, filter and permission expansion reset.
9. Confirm QA Sandbox Send and Save As Local still work; Cloud Save remains denied for QA.

Important: `View access` displays authoritative server-supplied permissions. Visual controls do not grant access; backend authorization remains the source of truth.
